AN ORIGINAL GAME CONCEPT

LIVE IN FOOTSTEPS

A survival game where your footsteps become civilization.

Not 4X. Still civilization.

See how it grows ↓

THE CORE IDEA

You live. Humanity follows.

You are a giant traveler, not a god. You do not place cities or command nations. You survive, move on, and leave traces behind. Humanity turns those traces into history.

01

YOU WALK.

They watch.

You travel for water, food and a place to rest. Human groups observe the routes you open through the landscape.

02

YOU REST.

They gather.

A campfire, a safe resting place, a fallen tree, a footprint — ordinary survival actions become opportunities for the people around you.

03

YOU LEAVE.

Civilization grows.

You keep traveling. Behind you, camps become villages, roads connect settlements, and centuries of human history unfold.

NOT 4X. STILL CIVILIZATION.

Build nothing directly.
Change everything indirectly.

The joy is not in managing every city. It is in seeing what people do with the world you accidentally helped shape.

Terrain, resources, movement and time decide which settlements thrive, decline, connect or transform — often long after you have left.

THE EXPERIENCE

Survival becomes world history.

01

Survive

Find water, food, shelter and places to sleep.

02

Explore

Follow curiosity instead of quest markers.

03

Leave traces

Your movement changes routes, resources and gathering places.

04

Watch history

Return centuries later and discover what humanity made of them.

THE LONG ARC

At first, humanity follows your footsteps.
In the end, you follow theirs.

As humanity develops roads, ships, bridges and railways, it no longer needs the giant's paths. The main journey ends when civilization becomes independent — but you can keep walking through the world it built.
