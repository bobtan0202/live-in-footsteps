LIVE IN FOOTSTEPS

A survival game where your footsteps become civilization.

Not 4X. Still civilization.

Discover the concept ↓

THE CORE LOOP

You live. Humanity follows.

01

YOU WALK.

They watch.

You travel to survive. Small human groups observe the paths, crossings, and traces you leave behind.

02

YOU REST.

They gather.

You stop for water, food and sleep. People gather around the safe places you happened to choose.

03

YOU LEAVE.

Civilization grows.

Years pass. Settlements become roads, ports and cities while you keep moving toward the horizon.

THE IDEA

Don’t build civilization.
Live — and let it grow.

You are a giant traveler, not a god. You do not place cities, assign citizens, choose technologies or order wars.

You walk, eat, drink, rest and survive. Humanity uses what you leave behind — footprints, campfires, fallen trees, opened routes and safe resting places — and turns those traces into history.

WHY IT FEELS DIFFERENT

Civilization without the management burden.

01

Survival first

Early play is about water, food, rest and exploration.

02

Emergent history

Human society develops on its own from terrain, resources and connections.

03

Minimal control

You influence the world through your life rather than through menus.

04

Every run is a world

Random geography, other giants and human movement create different histories.

THE LONG ARC

At first, humanity follows your footsteps.
In the end, you follow theirs.

Humanity eventually builds its own roads, ships, bridges and railways. The age of giants ends not with death, but with independence. You can keep walking through the world that grew from the traces you once left behind.

POSITIONING

For players who love worlds that tell their own stories.

Civilization / 4X

The joy of watching civilization develop — without directly managing every city and system.

RimWorld / simulation

Unexpected history emerges from autonomous people, geography and long-term consequences.

Survival / exploration

The player remains a physical traveler inside the world rather than an all-seeing controller above it.
